## Authenticated Exam Structure

### Part 1: 8 Announcements & Instructions (Q1–Q8)
- **Q1**: Haircut appointment day (Key: B - Thursday)
- **Q2**: Aunt's postcard scene (Key: C - Behind tall trees)
- **Q3**: Milan flight departure time (Key: C - 08:15)
- **Q4**: Mother's birthday present (Key: C - Pen)
- **Q5**: Apple pie baking completion time (Key: C - Twenty past five)
- **Q6**: Swimming lesson time today (Key: B - Fifteen to five)
- **Q7**: Boy's favorite subject (Key: C - Sports)
- **Q8**: T-shirt purchase choice (Key: A - Short-sleeved round neck)

### Part 2: 3 Conversations (Q9–Q20)
- **Conversation 1 (Q9–Q12)**: Kate and George (Mr. Gray lecture, cough medicine). Keys: 9A, 10B, 11C, 12D.
- **Conversation 2 (Q13–Q16)**: Evening leisure dilemma (Cinema vs. home video). Keys: 13A, 14B, 15C, 16D.
- **Conversation 3 (Q17–Q20)**: Lisa and Ben holiday plans (Scotland walking tour). Keys: 17A, 18B, 19C, 20D.

### Part 3: 3 Academic Talks / Lectures (Q21–Q35)
- **Talk 1 (Q21–Q25)**: Vietnamese immigrant family in New York. Keys: 21B, 22C, 23C, 24A, 25B.
- **Talk 2 (Q26–Q30)**: Single-sex vs. co-education in US schools (Title IX). Keys: 26D, 27C, 28B, 29A, 30D.
- **Talk 3 (Q31–Q35)**: Non-verbal communication & American body language. Keys: 31B, 32D, 33D, 34D, 35C.
