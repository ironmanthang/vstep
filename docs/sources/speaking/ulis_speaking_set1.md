# ULIS VSTEP Speaking Test Set 1

## Source Information
- **Institution**: Trung tâm Khảo thí, Trường Đại học Ngoại ngữ – Đại học Quốc gia Hà Nội (ULIS – VNU).
- **Exam Title**: Đề thi minh họa Đánh giá Năng lực Ngoại ngữ VSTEP.3–5.
- **Official Portal**: [vstep.vnu.edu.vn](http://vstep.vnu.edu.vn)
- **Time Allowed**: ~12 minutes (Preparation + Speaking).
- **Format**: 3 Parts (Social Interaction, Solution Discussion, Topic Development).

---

## Parts Breakdown

### Part 1: Social Interaction (3 minutes)
- **Topic 1: Free Time Activities**
  - What do you usually do in your spare time?
  - Do you prefer spending your free time indoors or outdoors? Why?
  - Do you think people have enough free time nowadays compared to the past?
- **Topic 2: Hometown**
  - Where is your hometown located?
  - What do you like most about your hometown?
  - How has your hometown changed over the last 10 years?

### Part 2: Solution Discussion (4 minutes)
- **Scenario**:
  > You and your university study group have won a 10-million VND academic prize. You are deciding how to spend this money:
  > - **Option A**: Organizing a weekend team celebration dinner.
  > - **Option B**: Buying shared English reference books and study materials.
  > - **Option C**: Saving the money for a future field trip project.
- **Task**: Choose the best option for your group, explain your reasons, and refute why the other two options are less suitable.

### Part 3: Topic Development (5 minutes)
- **Central Topic**: *Online learning brings significant advantages to university students.*
  - **Idea 1**: Flexible learning schedule and self-paced progress.
  - **Idea 2**: Access to diverse global resources and international courses.
  - **Idea 3**: Cost and travel time savings.
  - **[Your Own Idea]**: Candidate develops an additional point.
- **Follow-up Questions**:
  - What are the major drawbacks of online learning compared to traditional face-to-face classrooms?
  - How can teachers keep students engaged during virtual lectures?
