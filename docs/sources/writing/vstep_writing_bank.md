# Authentic VSTEP Writing Practice Bank

## Source Information
- **Source**: MOET VSTEP Authentic Exam Archive & Benchmark Model Responses
- **Exam Standards**: Định dạng Đánh giá Năng lực Ngoại ngữ VSTEP.3–5 (Bộ Giáo dục và Đào tạo)
- **Active Code Module**: `src/features/writing/data/writingBank.ts` (`WRITING_TASK1_BANK`, `WRITING_TASK2_BANK`)
- **Structure**: Task 1 (Letter/Email, ~120 words, 20 mins) + Task 2 (Academic Essay, ~250 words, 40 mins)

## Task 1 Practice Prompts (Letters / Emails)

### Letter 01: Course Evaluation & Feedback (`vstep_w_t1_01`)
- **Type**: Formal Letter / Feedback
- **Prompt**: Write a letter to the center director (Mr. Davis) evaluating a completed 3-month English speaking course at Sunrise Language Center, expressing gratitude, suggesting constructive improvements (duration and peer feedback), and proposing advanced weekend workshops.
- **Word Target**: Minimum 120 words
- **Benchmark Sample**: B2 Model Response included with detailed Vietnamese stylistic and lexical breakdown.

### Letter 02: Job Application for Community Sports Assistant (`vstep_w_t1_02`)
- **Type**: Formal Letter / Job Application
- **Prompt**: Apply for a part-time Assistant Coordinator role at City Youth Community Sports Club, stating relevant athletic leadership, physical education studies, VSTEP B2 certification, and scheduling availability.
- **Word Target**: Minimum 120 words
- **Benchmark Sample**: B2 Model Response included with organizational and functional phrasing analysis.

### Letter 03: Accommodation Inquiry & Booking (`vstep_w_t1_03`)
- **Type**: Semi-Formal Letter / Transactional Inquiry
- **Prompt**: Write to Seaside Holiday Apartments inquiring about booking a two-bedroom apartment for a family vacation, requesting details on dates, cooking amenities, and airport shuttle arrangements.
- **Word Target**: Minimum 120 words
- **Benchmark Sample**: B2 Model Response included with courtesy and inquiry conventions.

## Task 2 Practice Prompts (Academic Essays)

### Essay 01: Online Learning vs Traditional Classroom Education (`vstep_w_t2_01`)
- **Type**: Discussion / Opinion Essay
- **Prompt**: Discuss whether digital e-learning platforms can fully substitute traditional face-to-face classroom education, analyzing flexibility and self-paced progress versus social interaction and practical feedback.
- **Word Target**: Minimum 250 words
- **Benchmark Sample**: B2 Model Response structured into a 4-paragraph essay (Introduction, Counter-argument & Benefits of Online Learning, Superiority of In-person Dynamics, Balanced Conclusion).

### Essay 02: Public Transport Investment vs Road Expansion (`vstep_w_t2_02`)
- **Type**: Argumentative / Problem-Solution Essay
- **Prompt**: Analyze whether municipal governments should prioritize funding mass transit networks (subways, electric bus fleets) or expand road networks and highways to accommodate private vehicles.
- **Word Target**: Minimum 250 words
- **Benchmark Sample**: B2 Model Response analyzing urban gridlock, greenhouse emissions, long-term sustainability, and public expenditure efficiency.
