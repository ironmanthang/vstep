# ULIS VSTEP Writing Test Set 1

## Source Information
- **Institution**: Trung tâm Khảo thí, Trường Đại học Ngoại ngữ – Đại học Quốc gia Hà Nội (ULIS – VNU).
- **Exam Title**: Đề thi minh họa Đánh giá Năng lực Ngoại ngữ VSTEP.3–5.
- **Official Portal**: [vstep.vnu.edu.vn](http://vstep.vnu.edu.vn)
- **Time Allowed**: 60 minutes.
- **Format**: 2 Tasks (Task 1: Letter/Email, Task 2: Academic Essay).

---

## Tasks Breakdown

### Task 1: Letter / Email (1/3 total Writing score)
- **Suggested Time**: 20 minutes.
- **Word Requirement**: At least 120 words.
- **Prompt**:
  > You received an email from your English friend, David, who is planning to visit your city next month. He wants some advice about where to stay, what local places to visit, and what weather to prepare for.
  > Write an email to David. In your email:
  > - Welcome him and express excitement about his visit.
  > - Suggest suitable accommodation options (hotels, homestays).
  > - Recommend 2–3 must-visit attractions and local food specialties.
  > - Advise him on weather conditions and appropriate clothing.

### Task 2: Academic Essay (2/3 total Writing score)
- **Suggested Time**: 40 minutes.
- **Word Requirement**: At least 250 words.
- **Prompt**:
  > Some people argue that university students should focus strictly on academic subjects related to their chosen career, while others believe they should also study a wide range of extracurricular and creative subjects.
  > Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.
