## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: TV Review: Summer Broadcast Schedules (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: Media & Entertainment
- **Word Count**: ~485 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: Lucy Chang's critical television review on summer broadcast line-ups, critique of SuperTV repetitive game shows and recycled chat shows, comparison with Channel 9 historical series and documentaries, and the social role of television beyond pure escapist entertainment.

### Passage 2: The Reluctant Hero: Aaron Green's Breakthrough (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Film & Celebrity / Cinema
- **Word Count**: ~516 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Young actor Aaron Green's casting as the lead in an upcoming fantasy blockbuster, his sincere aversion to celebrity culture, background portraying a real-life paralyzed sports hero, emotional encounters with living subjects, and directors' admiration for his thoughtful professional dedication.

### Passage 3: Advertising: Art or Visual Pollution? (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Advertising & Urban Environment / Media Studies
- **Word Count**: ~539 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Psychological immersion of pervasive daily advertising (2,000–5,000 exposures daily), contrast between Tokyo's vibrant high-tech commercial advertising and historic temples, Mayor Gilberto Kassab's radical 2007 billboard ban in São Paulo (Clean City Law), commercial sector resistance, and public enthusiasm.

### Passage 4: Human Settlement of Australia & Lake Mungo Discoveries (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Anthropology & Australian Archaeology
- **Word Count**: ~593 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Evolution of archaeological estimates for human arrival in Australia (from 400 years to 8,000 years to Jim Bowler's 1969 Lake Mungo 23,000-year skeleton finding, and modern dates exceeding 45,000–60,000 years), deep water crossing challenges across the Wallace Line, and recognition of Australian Aborigines' ancient civilization.
