## Source Information

## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: Doctors treat patients for free at clinic in Ha Noi (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: Community & Social Work
- **Word Count**: ~427 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: Factual details, vocabulary in context, sentence insertion ([A]-[D]), doctor ethics, and patient testimony.

### Passage 2: To Get a Job in Your 50s, Maintain Friendships in Your 40s (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Employment & Aging
- **Word Count**: ~454 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Main purpose, statistical details, pronoun reference, vocabulary in context, author attitude, and advice evaluation.

### Passage 3: Mary's First Job (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Personal Memoir & Work
- **Word Count**: ~393 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Narrative details, shared workplace challenges, paraphrase recognition, vocabulary in context, and author impression.

### Passage 4: Potash Production and Colonial North America (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Industrial History & Science
- **Word Count**: ~353 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Chemical processes, industrial utility, negative detail (EXCEPT), technological comparison, and historical inference.
