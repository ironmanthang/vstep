# ULIS VSTEP Reading Test Set 4

## Source Information
- **Institution**: Trung tâm Khảo thí, Trường Đại học Ngoại ngữ – Đại học Quốc gia Hà Nội (ULIS – VNU).
- **Publication**: "7 Vstep Tests B1-B2-C1 Full Key" (NXB ĐHQGHN, 2019, Trang 46–51, PDF trang 50–55).
- **Answer Key**: Trang 145 của sách gốc (PDF page 145).
- **Format**: 4 Reading Passages (1,217 words total) / 40 Multiple-choice Questions (60 phút).
- **Target Difficulty**: B1–C1.

---

## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: The Development of New York Apartment Buildings (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: Architecture & Housing
- **Word Count**: ~332 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: Late 19th-century New York apartment evolution, the Stuyvesant building, architect Richard Morris Hunt, 25x100 ft building lot constraints (Question 6 diagram), and transition to luxury Park Avenue residences.

### Passage 2: The Formation and Characteristics of Snowfall (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Meteorology & Science
- **Word Count**: ~277 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Microscopic ice crystal nucleation, atmospheric vapor pressure dynamics, continuous moisture requirements, Pacific/Gulf/Atlantic moisture currents, and Great Lakes lake-effect storms.

### Passage 3: Social Parasitism in Ants (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Animal Behavior & Science
- **Word Count**: ~331 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Social parasitism in vertebrates (cuckoos/cowbirds) vs. dulotic ants, *Polyergus* specialization, raiding *Formica* colonies to capture pupal broods, mixed-colony labor division, and nest relocation logistics.

### Passage 4: The Winterthur Museum (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Art & Museums
- **Word Count**: ~277 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Country house museum concept, historical family residence atmosphere, decorative arts preservation, and evolutionary development of period-room display techniques.
