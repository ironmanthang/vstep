# ULIS VSTEP Reading Test Set 3

## Source Information
- **Institution**: Trung tâm Khảo thí, Trường Đại học Ngoại ngữ – Đại học Quốc gia Hà Nội (ULIS – VNU).
- **Publication**: "7 Vstep Tests B1-B2-C1 Full Key" (NXB ĐHQGHN, 2019, Trang 36–43).
- **Answer Key**: Trang 140 của sách gốc (PDF page 140).
- **Format**: 4 Reading Passages (1,430 words total) / 40 Multiple-choice Questions (60 phút).
- **Target Difficulty**: B1–C1.

---

## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: Phillis Wheatley (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: Biography & History
- **Word Count**: ~368 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: Historical inference, factual details, publisher intervention, publication timeline, and slavery context.

### Passage 2: Colonial Furniture Making in New England (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Architecture & Craftsmanship
- **Word Count**: ~354 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Woodworking joinery, material origins, hardware utilization, architectural expansion, and technical terminology.

### Passage 3: The Role of Nineteenth-Century Forts in the American West (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Military History & Frontier Life
- **Word Count**: ~357 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Frontier communication routes, military logistics, settlement protection, agricultural practices, and epidemic obstacles.

### Passage 4: Fossil Mineralization and Composition (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Paleontology & Chemistry
- **Word Count**: ~351 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Bone mineralization, hydroxyapatite durability, collagen decay, cellular permineralization, and noncollagen protein detection.
