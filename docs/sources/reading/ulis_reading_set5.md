## Source Information
- **Institution**: Trung tâm Khảo thí, Trường Đại học Ngoại ngữ – Đại học Quốc gia Hà Nội (ULIS – VNU).

- **Answer Key**: Trang 145 của sách gốc (PDF page 149).
- **Format**: 4 Reading Passages (1,715 words total) / 40 Multiple-choice Questions (60 phút).
- **Target Difficulty**: B1–C1.

---

## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: ASEAN Summit and AEC Launch (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: International Relations & Economy
- **Word Count**: ~420 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: ASEAN Economic Community (AEC) inauguration on Dec 31, single market integration, Paris attacks security focus at the November leaders summit, customs project single-window trials, and migrant worker protections raised by civil society groups.

### Passage 2: Space Tourism and Dennis Tito (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Aerospace & Tourism
- **Word Count**: ~440 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Neil Armstrong 1969 milestone vs. Dennis Tito 2001 first civilian space flight, Mir space station postponement, Star City Gagarin Cosmonauts training, NASA liability agreements and restrictions, and market surveys on space vacation willingness in Japan and North America.

### Passage 3: The Pullman Sleeping Car and American Railroad History (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Transportation & History
- **Word Count**: ~430 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Pre-Civil War railroad challenges, George M. Pullman's oversized Pioneer sleeping car, breakthrough role in President Abraham Lincoln's 1865 funeral train route from Springfield to Chicago, General Grant's subsequent adoption, and company evolution.

### Passage 4: Waste Management Methods (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Environmental Science & Waste Management
- **Word Count**: ~425 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Four fundamental waste processing methods (recycling, land-filling, composting, incinerating), energy balance in recycling, groundwater toxic runoff and compaction in landfills, plastic separation constraints in municipal composting, and energy recovery vs. emissions in incineration.
