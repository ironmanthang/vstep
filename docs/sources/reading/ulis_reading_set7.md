## Passages Breakdown

### Passage 1: Take me out to the Ballpark (Q1–Q10)
- **Topic**: Sports & Culture
- **Word Count**: ~502 words
- **Difficulty**: B1
- **Focus**: Baseball as the American national pastime and its growing global spread across South America, Japan, and Europe due to Olympic status. Cultural traditions of ballpark spectatorship (hot dogs, summer stands, childhood memories), game history in New York, equipment (bat, ball, leather glove, lightweight uniform), field layout (infield diamond with three bases, outfield grass), nine innings structure with pitcher/batting mechanics, and fast-paced moves. Q5 features authentic sentence insertion ([A], [B], [C], [D]).

### Passage 2: The Decathlon: Ten Events, One Champion (Q11–Q20)
- **Topic**: Athletics & Sports
- **Word Count**: ~586 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: Historical evolution of multi-event athletic challenges from the ancient Greek Olympic pentathlon (708 BC first winner Lampis of Sparta, banned in 393 AD by Emperor Theodosius) to the modern decathlon introduced at the 1912 Stockholm Olympics and women's heptathlon. Interview with American decathlete Bruce Thorpe explaining two-day event breakdown (Day 1: 100m, long jump, shot put, high jump, 400m; Day 2: 110m hurdles, discus, javelin, pole vault, 1500m), physical demands, stamina challenges of the 1500m, and training advice.

### Passage 3: Coast to Coast: Australia on a Skateboard (Q21–Q30)
- **Topic**: Personal Memoir & Adventure
- **Word Count**: ~560 words
- **Difficulty**: B2
- **Focus**: 27-year-old English graphic designer David Cornthwaite's record-breaking 5,800-kilometre skateboard journey across Australia from Perth to Brisbane. Background quitting his unfulfilling job, training across Britain (John O'Groats to Lands End), severe physical hardships (road train windblasts, blisters, 40°C heat, 13 pairs of shoes, calf hypertrophy), support team logistics, breaking Jack Smith's 2003 US record, a fall 3km before the finish, and post-journey plans (surfing on the Gold Coast, writing a book, motivational speaking).

### Passage 4: On Tour with the London Symphony Orchestra (Q31–Q40)
- **Topic**: Music & Careers
- **Word Count**: ~389 words
- **Difficulty**: C1
- **Focus**: Parallels between professional footballers and orchestra musicians drawn by Rod Franks (former Leeds United footballer turned principal trumpeter of the LSO). Challenges of playing abroad, managing director Clive Gillinson's commercial strategy for international reputation and recording contracts, event branding through festival projects, tour administrator Sue Mallet's complex logistical planning (flight delays, snowbound instrument lorries), and emotional artistic rewards of international acclaim.
